Question: Can a satellite image of the Chirchik basin tell me how much water reaches my field near Tashkent? What data would you need?

Snow2Crop answer: Delivery to a particular field is unknown. Releases, withdrawals and losses along the route, or measured delivery to the field, are needed. Assessing a shortage also requires demand for the same period.
